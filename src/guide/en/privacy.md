# Privacy

SharpMD has no trackers and no third parties. The files you open are read in your browser and are not uploaded. Settings, reading positions and browser notes are stored in your browser. The site and the web app count a few anonymous events, such as a visit or the app opened, to know whether the product works: no cookie, no identifier and nothing about your notes. You turn it off in **Settings** > **Advanced**, and the extension sends none.

The cloud is optional: a note reaches the server only when you send it there. Cloud notes are stored encrypted, with a key the server holds so it can share them and serve your AI. A folder with a password is encrypted in your browser and the server cannot read it. The AI assistant sends the text straight from your browser to the provider you chose, and dictation uses the speech recognition of the browser: SharpMD receives neither your key, your text nor your audio.

The details are on the [privacy page](https://sharpmd.app/privacy.html).
