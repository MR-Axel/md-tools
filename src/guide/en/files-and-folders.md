# Files and folders

The explorer is in the sidebar, under the outline. It shows where each note comes from.

| Where | What it keeps | Where you see it |
|---|---|---|
| A folder on disk | Your files, on your computer. They are not uploaded | On this machine |
| **In this browser** | Notes that save themselves, with no account and no folder | In this browser |
| **Cloud** | The notes you sent to your account | On any device you sign in on |

## A folder on disk

**Open folder** shows the file tree. From there you create, rename, delete and move files and folders by dragging them. Search goes through every file, and `[[name]]` links to another note in the folder.

If another program creates or deletes a file, the explorer notices on its own within seconds.

Chrome, Edge and Brave open folders and save in place. Firefox and Safari open one file at a time, and saving downloads a copy.

What you already opened stays under **Recent**.

## In this browser

**New note** saves here. If you would rather have new notes go to a folder on disk, pick it with **Save them to a folder**, on the start screen.

> [!WARNING]
> Safari can delete the notes kept in the browser after weeks without use unless the app is installed. Install the app or use the cloud.

## A folder's page

A click on the name of a folder opens its page: the index of its subfolders and notes, with the description on top. The description is the folder's `README.md`, and **Add a description** creates it and opens it for writing. The triangle unfolds the folder without opening it. On a phone the page opens with **Open**, in the folder's menu. The page has its own address: it can be bookmarked, and **Copy the link** copies it.

## Sending to the cloud

- The cloud button, in the top bar, uploads the open note. A browser note moves to the cloud. With a file on disk you choose between **Move to the cloud** and **Keep a copy**.
- In the explorer, a right-click on a file or a folder offers **Send to the cloud**. It uploads a copy and what is on your disk does not change.
- On a cloud folder, the same menu offers **Upload files** and **Upload folder**.

The cloud keeps notes, which are text. A PDF or a book is not uploaded: you view it from the disk.

Next: [The cloud and sharing](cloud-and-sharing.md).

## More than Markdown

- PDF and EPUB open in a built-in reader, by pages or chapters, with an outline and search. It remembers where you were.
- Images open in a viewer with zoom.
- Audio and video open in the browser's player.
- Code and config files show highlighted, and a CSV shows as a table.
- A `.txt`, a `.json` or a `.yaml` is edited and saved in its own format.
- Word, Excel and PowerPoint offer **Import to Markdown**. See [Export and import](export-and-import.md).

The **Markdown only** switch in the explorer hides everything else.
