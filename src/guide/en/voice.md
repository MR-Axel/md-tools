# Dictation and read aloud

They are two tools. Both are turned on in **Settings** > **Tools**.

## Read aloud

It reads the whole note, from a block or only the selection, with the voices of your device. It marks the block and the sentence it is on and keeps them in view.

- A collapsed section opens while it is read and closes again.
- Code and diagrams are announced, not read.
- `Alt+Shift+S` starts and stops the reading.

## Dictation

You write by speaking, in English or Spanish. There are commands for punctuation, headings, lists, tasks and formatting: you say "period", "comma" or "new paragraph" and the mark goes in.

There are two more modes:

| You say | It builds |
|---|---|
| "formula" and, when done, "end formula" | A formula in LaTeX |
| "flowchart" and, when done, "end diagram" | A Mermaid flowchart |

Both are drawn while you speak. `Alt+Shift+D` starts and stops dictation.

> [!NOTE]
> Dictation uses the speech recognition of the browser, on the device when the browser offers it. SharpMD never receives audio.

It works in Chrome, Edge and Safari. Inside the Android app the microphone is not available: use the dictation of the keyboard there.

More on what leaves your device in [Privacy](privacy.md).
